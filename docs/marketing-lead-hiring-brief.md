# Marketing Lead/Specialist — Hiring Brief

## Role Overview
Marketing Lead for the TT Manager app (table tennis coaching platform). First marketing hire. Will own user acquisition, brand awareness, and growth for iOS and Android app launch.

## Candidate Profile

### Essential Requirements
- 3+ years mobile app marketing (iOS/Android)
- Experience with App Store Optimization (ASO), Apple Search Ads, Google Ads
- Passion for or knowledge of table tennis / racket sports
- SaaS product marketing experience (freemium or subscription models)
- Track record of measurable growth (MAU, retention, conversion metrics)

### Desirable
- Experience marketing coaching/education apps
- Influencer/creator partnership experience (YouTube coaches, social athletes)
- Content marketing and community building skills
- Familiarity with sports tech ecosystem (PingPod, TeamGenius, GameChanger)
- Experience in early-stage/startup environments

### Key Responsibilities (Day 1-90)
1. **ASO** — Optimize app store listings, keywords, screenshots, preview videos
2. **Pre-launch content** — Build social media presence (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram) focused on table tennis coaching content
3. **Influencer outreach** — Partner with table tennis coaches and YouTube creators (Anders Lind network, TableTennisDaily community)
4. **User acquisition** — Set up and manage paid channels: Apple Search Ads, Google App Campaigns, Meta ads
5. **Analytics** — Define KPIs, set up tracking (Appsflyer/Adjust or similar MMP)
6. **Launch strategy** — Plan app store launch campaigns, PR, and community engagement

## Salary Benchmark

1. **Screening call** (30 min) — Background, app marketing experience, sports knowledge
2. **Case study** — "How would you launch and acquire the first 1,000 users for a table tennis coaching app?"

4. **Reference checks** — Contact previous employers for growth results

## Competitor Analysis for Context

- **PingPod** — $19M raised, expanding via licensing. Software + franchise model
- **TeamGenius/GameChanger** — Youth sports tournament management. Strong community growth
