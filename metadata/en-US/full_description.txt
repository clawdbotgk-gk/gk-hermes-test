TT Manager is the ultimate table tennis tournament management app. Built by players, for players and coaches.

Create professional tournaments in seconds with four tournament formats:

- Knockout: Single elimination brackets
- Round Robin: Every player faces every player
- Double Knockout: Winners and losers brackets
- Groups + Knockout: Stage tournaments with group play followed by elimination

Live Score Tracking -- Submit scores game-by-game from any device, right from the table.

Key Features:
- Instant tournament setup
- Automatic bracket generation
- Real-time score updates
- Player management
- Match history tracking
- Export results

Whether you're running a local club meetup or a regional tournament, TT Manager handles it all.
